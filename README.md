The contents of this project reflect the ongoing collaboration between the U.S. Department of Agriculture's Agricultural Research Service (USDA-ARS) and the University of Texas at Arlington's (UTA) Water Engineering Research Center (WERC) during Summer 2026. The project is focused on developing tools that streamline HEC-HMS workflows, with the goal of improving the efficiency, consistency, and reproducibility of future HEC-HMS model development.

This work is continued by Jack Yohn, an undergraduate Computer Engineering researcher at UTA and a USDA-ARS Summer 2026 intern, under the guidance of Dr. Eddy Langendoen (USDA-ARS), Dr. Nick Fang, Director of UTA's Water Engineering Research Center, and Dr. Daniel Li (WERC). Additional support and collaboration provided by WERC graduate research assistants Kelli Greenwood and Binu Devkota.
